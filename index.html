Everything a good Sudoku needs

Simple to pick up, satisfying to master. No clutter, no guessing.

4 difficulty levels

From a relaxed Easy to a demanding Expert. Every puzzle is generated fresh, so you never run out.

One solution, always

Each puzzle has exactly one solution, so logic always works — you never have to guess.

Pencil notes

Jot down candidates in a cell. Notes clear themselves from related cells when you place a number.

Hints & undo

Three hints per game, unlimited undo and an optional three-mistake limit to keep it challenging.

Light & dark themes

Follows your system the first time. The board highlights the row, column, box and matching numbers.

Frequently asked questions

Is Sudoku Grid free?

Yes, Sudoku Grid is free to play.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No account is needed. The Android app works offline. The browser version needs a connection the first time it loads.

Where is my progress stored?

Only on your own device (in the app or in your browser). We do not receive your games or statistics. See the privacy policy.

Which devices are supported?

Any modern desktop or mobile browser, and Android phones and tablets (store releases are coming soon).

How are the puzzles made?

They are generated by the game and checked so that each one has exactly one solution.

How do I contact you?

Write to voidfontstudio@gmail.com.
