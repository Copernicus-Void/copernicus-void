Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-09

Summary. Sudoku Grid is a free puzzle game supported by advertising. We do not ask you for an account and we do not collect your name, email or location ourselves. The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob, which may collect certain device information and an advertising identifier to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland) you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time.

1. Who we are

Sudoku Grid is published by Copernicus Void ("we", "us"), established in Dominican Republic. For privacy questions or requests write to copernicusvoid@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

To let you continue later, the game saves the following on your own device (in the app's private storage or, in the online version, in your browser):

  • your game in progress,
  • your settings (theme, language, sound, vibration and display options),
  • simple statistics (games started and won, best times) and any extra hints or undos you earned.

We do not receive this information. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear your browser's site data.

3. What we do not collect

We do not operate any server that receives data from the app. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data, and the game has no user accounts, chat or social features. We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services of our own. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Android app is free and funded by ads served by Google AdMob (Google LLC, and Google Ireland Limited for users in the EEA and the UK). The app shows banner ads, full-screen ads when you start a new game (every second game) and optional rewarded videos that you can choose to watch to earn extra hints or undos.

Depending on the store or device (for example, versions distributed through Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets), the app may be offered without ads; in that case none of the advertising data collection described in this section takes place.

When the app requests an ad, the Google Mobile Ads SDK that is built into the app collects and uses information, which Google describes as including:

  • IP address (from which an approximate location may be derived);
  • device and account identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and an app-set ID;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language, the app's name and version;
  • ad interactions, such as impressions, clicks and video views;
  • diagnostics, such as crash logs and performance data.

Google uses this information to deliver, personalize (where you have allowed it or the law does not require consent) and measure ads, to prevent fraud and abuse, and to maintain and improve its services. For this purpose Google acts as an independent controller of the data it collects through its SDK. We, as the app publisher, only receive aggregated reports (such as number of ad views and earnings by country) and no information that identifies you.

More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

5. Your choices about ads

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland). The first time you open the app, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform lets you accept, refuse or manage ad personalization. You can review or change your choice at any time in the app: About → Ad privacy options (or Settings → Ad privacy options). If you do not give consent where it is required, ads are not requested.
  • Personalized and non-personalized ads. You can use Google's controls at adssettings.google.com to turn off ad personalization.
  • Android advertising ID. In your device settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads, wording varies by manufacturer) you can delete your advertising ID or reset it, and opt out of ads personalization.
  • United States and other regions. Serving ads based on your activity may be considered "targeted advertising" or "sharing" under some state privacy laws. You can opt out with the device and Google controls above or by writing to us.
  • No ads at all. If you prefer, you can play the browser version, which currently shows no ads.

6. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

We do not process your personal data ourselves. For ads, Google relies on your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and the ePrivacy rules on accessing information on your device) for personalized advertising and for storing or reading identifiers, which is collected through the consent form described above. You may withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing already carried out.

7. International transfers and retention

Google may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States, using safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or adequacy frameworks, as described in its terms. We keep no personal data about you. Data stored on your device remains until you uninstall the app or clear its data; Google's retention periods are set by Google.

8. Children

Sudoku Grid is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed to children under 13 (or under the higher age of digital consent that applies in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are under that age, please do not use the app without a parent or guardian, and parents who believe a child has used it can contact us.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, Brazil's LGPD, California's CCPA/CPRA and other laws) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, and not to be discriminated against for using these rights. Because we hold no personal data that identifies you, there is usually nothing for us to access or delete. Data Google collected through ads can be managed in your Google account at myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority (in the EEA, the supervisory authority of your country; in the UK, the ICO; in Switzerland, the FDPIC). If you are in the Dominican Republic, you may exercise your rights of access, rectification, cancellation and objection under Law No. 172-13 on the Comprehensive Protection of Personal Data, and other applicable local laws, by writing to us. To make any request, write to copernicusvoid@gmail.com. If we are ever required to appoint a representative in the EU or the UK (GDPR / UK GDPR Art. 27), we will publish their details here.

10. United States: state privacy notice

This section supplements this policy for residents of California (CCPA/CPRA) and other US states with privacy laws (such as Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas and others).

  • What is collected. Through the ad SDK, Google may collect these categories of personal information: identifiers (advertising ID, IP address), internet or network activity (ad interactions), approximate geolocation (derived from IP) and device information, in the last 12 months. We do not collect sensitive personal information and we do not collect personal information ourselves.
  • Sources and purposes. Collected directly from your device by Google for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention and service improvement. Disclosed to: Google, as our advertising service provider.
  • No sale for money; possible "sharing". We do not sell personal information in exchange for money. Showing ads based on your activity may be considered "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising or "targeted advertising". You can opt out at any time using your device's ad ID controls and Google's ad settings described above, or by emailing us with the subject "Do not sell or share".
  • Your rights. To know, access, correct and delete your personal information, to opt out of sale/sharing/targeted advertising, to limit the use of sensitive information (not applicable, as we collect none) and to not be discriminated against for exercising them. You may use an authorized agent. We respond within 45 days (extendable where the law allows). If we deny a request you may appeal by replying to us with the subject "Appeal"; you may also contact your state Attorney General.
  • Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control. Our website sets no cookies and does not track you across sites, so it does not change its behavior in response to Do Not Track signals. Opt-out settings in your device and Google account apply to the app's ads.
  • California "Shine the Light". We do not disclose personal information to third parties for their own direct-marketing purposes.
  • Children (COPPA). We do not direct the app to children under 13 and do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

11. Website and online version

This website and the browser version of the game are served by a hosting provider (currently GitHub Pages). Like any web server, hosting providers may process technical data such as your IP address and browser type in their logs under their own privacy policies. We do not use that data to identify you. The online version saves your progress in your browser's storage (IndexedDB / local storage); we do not set cookies. If you play on a third-party platform, that platform's privacy policy also applies.

The website and the online version currently do not show ads and use no analytics.

12. Security, links and changes

Security. Game data stays on your device, and our website is served over HTTPS. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Links. Ads and some pages link to third-party sites that have their own policies, for which we are not responsible. Changes. If we change this policy, for example because we add a new service, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, publish it before the new version of the app is released.
