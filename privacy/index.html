Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-09

Summary: Sudoku Grid does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell your personal data. You do not need an account, and the Android app works without an internet connection.

1. Who we are

Sudoku Grid is published by Copernicus Void ("we", "us"). You can contact us at voidfontstudio@gmail.com.

2. What the game stores on your device

To let you continue later, the game saves the following only on your own device (in the app's private storage, or in your browser when you play online):

  • your game in progress,
  • your settings (theme, language, sound, vibration and display options),
  • simple statistics (games started and won, best times).

This information is never sent to us. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear the site data of your browser.

3. What we do not collect

The current version of Sudoku Grid does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, files, device identifiers, advertising ID, analytics or crash reports.

4. The website and the online version

This website and the browser version of the game are served by a hosting provider (currently GitHub Pages). Like any web server, hosting providers may process technical data such as your IP address and browser type in their logs, under their own privacy policies. We do not use this data to identify you.

The online version saves your progress in your browser's storage (IndexedDB / local storage). We do not set cookies. If you play Sudoku Grid on a third-party platform such as itch.io, that platform's own privacy policy also applies.

5. Third-party services

The current version has no advertising, analytics or other third-party services. If we add any in the future (for example, advertising), we will update this policy before the new version is released and explain what is collected and how you can control it.

6. Children

Sudoku Grid is a general-audience puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Your choices

Because we do not hold personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access or delete. You can erase everything the game saved by uninstalling the app or clearing your browser's site data. If you believe we hold information about you, please contact us.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest revision.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to voidfontstudio@gmail.com.
