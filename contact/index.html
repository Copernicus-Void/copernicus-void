Help topics

Which devices are supported?

The Android app needs Android 7.0 or newer. The online version works in current versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari on desktop and mobile.

My game or progress disappeared

Progress is stored only on your device (or in your browser). It is lost if you uninstall the app, clear the app's data, change phone, or—on the web—clear site data or use a private window. We have no accounts and cannot restore it.

Why are there ads, and how often do they appear?

Ads keep the game free. A small banner sits at the bottom of the screen, and a full-screen ad appears when you start every second new game (the first game has no ad, the second does, and so on)—never during a game. Videos with a reward are always optional.

How do I get more hints or undos?

Each game has 3 hints and 3 undos. When you run out you can choose to watch a short video to earn 3 more, and you can do it again whenever you run out. If the button shows "+3" a video is available; if it is greyed out, try again in a moment or check your connection. The online version has no ads and unlimited undo.

No video is available

Videos depend on ad availability and your internet connection. Check your connection and try again later. Some regions may have fewer ads available.

I saw an inappropriate or misleading ad

Please tell us using the "Ad problem" button above with a screenshot, the time it appeared and your device. We can block advertisers and categories in our ad settings. You can also report an ad to Google from the ad's information (i) icon, where available.

How do I change my ad consent or reset my advertising ID?

Open Home → About → Ad privacy options (shown when your region requires it). To reset or delete your advertising ID, go to your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads (the wording varies by manufacturer). More in our Privacy Policy.

The online version does not save my game

Browsers in private mode, or with site data blocked, cannot save progress. Allow site data for this website, avoid private windows, and make sure you are using a current browser. Sound needs one click or tap on "Play" to start.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstall the app (or clear its data in Android settings). Because we do not collect personal data on any server, nothing else needs to be deleted. For data that Google collected through ads, use the controls in the Privacy Policy.

Sound, vibration, language or theme

Open Home → Settings. You can switch sound effects and vibration, dark/light theme and English/Español there.

Accessibility

We want the game to be comfortable for everyone. If something is hard to use (contrast, size, controls), tell us and we will look into it.
